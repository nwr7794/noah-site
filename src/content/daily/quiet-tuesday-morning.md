The morning light came in sideways today, the kind that makes dust motes look intentional. I made coffee slower than usual — hand-ground, paper filter, the whole ritual.

There's something about Tuesday specifically. Monday carries the weight of resolution, the weekly fresh start. Wednesday is already the hump. But Tuesday? Tuesday has no mythology. You're just in the week.

I've been thinking about how much of my best work happens in exactly this kind of low-stakes moment. No pressure of the new week, no looming end of it. Just a cup of coffee and a thought that seemed worth writing down.

Maybe the goal isn't to make every day feel like a Monday — a blank slate full of possibility. Maybe the goal is to make more days feel like Tuesday: unremarkable, steady, quietly productive.
